Nightstand by Switch Suite: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Nightstand by Switch Suite ("Nightstand", "the app") is a bedside clock app published by Switch Suite. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Nightstand doesn't collect your personal data.

What stays on your phone

Nightstand keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • your clock and red-mode settings;
  • your built-in alarm (time, repeat days, chosen sound, and a copy of that sound so the alarm can ring after a restart);
  • whether Nightstand Pro is active on your device.

To work, the app reads your phone's light sensor (for red night mode), battery level, and the next alarm set in your Clock app. These readings are used on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Switch Suite has no server for Nightstand. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Purchases

Nightstand Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; Switch Suite never sees your payment details. To find out whether you own Pro, the app asks Google Play on your device, and keeps only "Pro: yes/no" on your device.

The app uses Google's Play Billing Library for purchases. That library communicates with Google Play and may send diagnostic information about the purchase process to Google. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Nightstand is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://switchsuite.io/privacy/nightstand/ with a new "Last updated" date.
