Relay by Switch Suite: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Relay by Switch Suite ("Relay", "the app") is a Mac app published by Switch Suite. It keeps lists you choose in Apple Reminders on your Mac in sync with your Google Tasks. Switch Suite has no server for Relay and doesn't receive your lists, your Google account or any other personal data. Your lists move only between your Mac, Apple's Reminders service (which Apple syncs through iCloud if you use it) and your Google Tasks.

What Relay reads and writes

With your permission (macOS asks once), Relay reads and updates only the Reminders lists you pair. For each item it syncs the title, notes, whether it's completed, and the due date. It doesn't read or change any other list, or other details such as alarms, priority, flags, tags, locations, URLs or attachments.

When you sign in with Google, Relay asks Google only for access to Google Tasks. It reads and updates the Google Tasks lists you pair, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) directly between your Mac and Google.

The sync marker

So that two Macs don't sync the same list at once, Relay keeps one completed task named "Relay sync marker (keep this completed)" in each Google Tasks list you pair. Its notes contain a random ID for your Mac, your Mac's name (as set in System Settings → General → Sharing) and the time it last synced. It's visible under Completed in Google Tasks. Removing the pair doesn't delete it; you can delete it yourself.

What's stored on your Mac

Relay keeps the following only on your Mac:

  • your settings and which lists are paired;
  • a sync database, so it knows what changed since the last sync. It contains the titles, notes, completion and due dates of synced items;
  • a log of recent sync activity, which includes item titles;
  • your Google sign-in token, in the macOS keychain, kept on this Mac only (it isn't synced to other devices).

The settings, database and log are stored in Relay's own private app storage on your Mac.

How Relay uses Google user data

Relay uses the data it gets through Google Tasks only to keep your paired lists in sync, as you set them up. It doesn't send that data to Switch Suite or anyone else. It doesn't use it for advertising, and it doesn't use it to train AI or machine-learning models. Relay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy

Sharing, transfer, and disclosure of Google user data

  • What Relay accesses. Relay accesses your Google Tasks data only to sync the lists you chose to pair.
  • Where it goes. Google user data is transferred only between your own Mac and Google's servers, and to Apple Reminders (and iCloud, if you use it) on your own devices, to perform the sync you asked for.
  • No sharing with anyone else. Relay and Switch Suite do not share, sell, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. Switch Suite itself never receives it: there is no Switch Suite server.
  • No advertising, data brokers or AI training. Google user data is never used for advertising, never given or sold to data brokers or other information resellers, and never used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Legal requirements. The only exception would be a disclosure required by law. None occurs in normal operation, and because Switch Suite never receives Google user data, it has none to disclose.
  • Your sign-in token. The OAuth token Relay uses to reach Google Tasks is stored in the macOS keychain on your Mac.
  • Revoking access. You can revoke Relay's access at any time: Settings → Google → Sign Out in Relay, or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Purchases

Relay Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Switch Suite never sees your payment details. To check whether you own Relay Pro, the app asks the App Store on your Mac and keeps only "Pro: yes/no" on your Mac.

What Relay doesn't do

Relay has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no Switch Suite account. Its alerts are notifications on your Mac; nothing is emailed or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Google → Sign Out removes Relay's Google sign-in token from your keychain. You can also remove Relay's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing a pair stops syncing those lists. Your items in Reminders and Google Tasks stay as they are.
  • Deleting the app and its data (in the Finder, or with an uninstaller) deletes everything Relay stored on your Mac.
  • Relay changes items in Reminders or Google Tasks only to mirror changes you made on the other side, including deletions, as you chose when you paired the lists ("Mark it completed" or "Delete it too"). If a sync would remove a lot of items at once, Relay stops and asks you first.

Children

Relay is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://switchsuite.io/privacy/relay/ with a new "Last updated" date.
