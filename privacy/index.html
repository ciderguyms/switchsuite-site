Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy covers the Switch Suite app for Android (package io.switchsuite.hub), published on Google Play by Switch Suite.

Summary

Switch Suite does not collect, store, share, sell or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not have permission to access the internet.

What the app does on your device

Switch Suite shows the Switch Suite family of apps and whether each one is installed on your phone, so it can offer to open it or show its Google Play page. To do that it asks Android whether these specific packages are installed:

  • io.switchsuite.courier
  • io.switchsuite.nightstand
  • io.switchsuite.relay
  • io.switchsuite.deskthread

It cannot see any other app on your phone. The result is used only to draw the screen. It is not saved and never leaves your device.

Links that leave the app

When you tap Install, Rate, Privacy Policy or Contact Support, Switch Suite hands the link to another app on your phone (Google Play, your browser or your email app). What happens next is governed by that app's and that service's own privacy policy. Switch Suite receives nothing back.

switchsuite.io is a static website. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
