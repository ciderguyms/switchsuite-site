DeskThread by Switch Suite: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

DeskThread by Switch Suite ("DeskThread", "the app") is a Mac app published by Switch Suite. It works with Google Messages: it shows Google Messages for web (messages.google.com) in its own window and adds Mac features such as native notifications, a Dock badge and, optionally, copying verification codes. Switch Suite has no server for DeskThread and never receives, stores or sees your messages, contacts or any other personal data.

How messaging works

Your messages travel between your phone, Google's servers and the Google Messages web page that DeskThread displays, exactly as they do when you use Google Messages for web in a browser. Google handles the messaging and your pairing with your phone; Google's privacy policy applies to that. DeskThread doesn't change, copy or forward that traffic.

What DeskThread does on your Mac

Everything below happens only on your Mac:

  • Showing the web page. The page's sign-in state (cookies and website data) is kept in DeskThread's private app container, so you stay paired after you quit and reopen it. It's the same kind of data a browser keeps.
  • Notifications. When the page reports a new message, DeskThread shows it as a macOS notification. With "Show message preview" on, the notification shows the sender and the message; with it off, it says only "New message". macOS keeps delivered notifications in Notification Center until you clear them.
  • Dock badge. The unread count is read from the page's title (for example "(3)") and shown on the Dock icon.
  • Verification codes (optional, off by default, DeskThread Pro). If you turn on "Copy verification codes automatically", DeskThread checks each incoming notification's text on your Mac for a one-time code and, if it finds one, copies just the code to the clipboard. The copy is marked as concealed and transient, so clipboard managers that honor those markers don't save it, and DeskThread clears it after the time you choose (30–120 seconds) if you haven't copied anything else meanwhile.
  • Attachments you download are saved to your Downloads folder. Files you attach are read only when you pick or drag them in.

DeskThread doesn't log, store or send message text, contacts or codes. It keeps only its settings (such as whether previews and code copying are on) and the page's website data, all in its app container on your Mac.

What DeskThread doesn't do

DeskThread has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no Switch Suite account. It doesn't use your messages for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models. Links to other websites in your messages open in your default browser.

Purchases

DeskThread Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Switch Suite never sees your payment details. To check whether you own Pro, the app asks the App Store on your Mac and keeps only "Pro: yes/no" on your Mac.

Deleting your data

  • Unpair: sign out of Google Messages for web inside DeskThread (the page's menu), or remove the computer from your phone's Google Messages app (Device pairing). This ends the web session.
  • Notifications: clear them in Notification Center, or turn DeskThread's notifications off in System Settings → Notifications.
  • Everything else: quit DeskThread and delete the app and its container folder ~/Library/Containers/io.switchsuite.deskthread/. That removes its settings and the page's website data from your Mac.

Children

DeskThread is not directed at children.
