Couch by Switch Suite: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Couch by Switch Suite ("Couch", "the app") is a TV remote app, compatible with Apple TV, published by Switch Suite. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Couch doesn't collect your personal data. Everything stays on your phone and your home network.

How Couch works

Couch finds TVs on your Wi-Fi network and talks to them directly over your local network, the same way a remote app on a phone does. It sends your button presses, swipes, typing and app choices to the TV, and receives back what the TV reports: whether a text field is open, whether the TV is on, the list of apps on the TV, and, if you turn on Now Playing, what is playing (app, title, artist or show, progress and artwork).

None of this goes through the internet or to Switch Suite. Switch Suite has no server for Couch. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account.

What stays on your phone

Couch keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • the names of the TVs you paired;
  • the pairing keys for each TV, encrypted with a key held in Android's Keystore (they are never backed up or transferred to another phone);
  • your settings (touchpad or buttons, notification controls);
  • whether Couch Pro is active on your device.

What the TV reports (apps, what's playing, artwork) is shown while you use the app and is not saved. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. To remove a pairing from the TV itself, use the TV's settings for remotes and devices.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / local network: to find TVs on your Wi-Fi and connect to them. Couch doesn't use it to find your location.
  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on Controls in Notification.
  • Connected-device foreground service (optional, Pro): only if you turn on Controls in Notification with Now Playing set up. It keeps the connection to your TV open while your phone is locked, so the lock screen can show what's playing and its controls work. A notification is shown the whole time; turning the setting off stops it.
  • Network access: to talk to your TV on your local network, and for Google Play purchases.

Purchases

Couch Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; Switch Suite never sees your payment details. To find out whether you own Pro, the app asks Google Play on your device, and keeps only "Pro: yes/no" on your device.

The app uses Google's Play Billing Library for purchases. That library communicates with Google Play and may send diagnostic information about the purchase process to Google. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Couch is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
