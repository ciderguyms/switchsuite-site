Frequently asked questions

Where can I download the apps?

Switch Suite and its utilities are coming soon to Google Play. This page will link to each one when it is released.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones, tablets and foldables running Android 12 or later.

Is the Switch Suite app free?

Yes. The Switch Suite app is free, with no ads. Some utilities may offer optional one-time upgrades, shown clearly in the app before you buy.

Does Switch Suite collect my data?

No. The Switch Suite app collects no personal data. Each utility has its own privacy policy; see Privacy.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account, open the utility's settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Email support@switchsuite.io with the app name, its version (shown in the app's settings) and your phone model.
