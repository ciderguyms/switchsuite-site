Relay by Switch Suite

Relay by Switch Suite is a Mac menu-bar app that keeps the lists you choose in Apple Reminders on your Mac in sync with your Google Tasks, so the same lists are on your Android phone. Add, edit, complete or delete an item on either side and Relay makes the same change on the other side, usually within a minute.

Relay asks Google only for access to Google Tasks, and uses it only to keep the lists you pair in sync. Your lists go directly between your Mac, Apple's Reminders service and Google Tasks; Switch Suite has no server for Relay and receives none of your data. Your Google sign-in is kept in your Mac's keychain.

About

Switch Suite is a family of small, focused utilities for people moving to a new phone ecosystem. Each utility has a familiar, minimalist design and is a separate app: the Android utilities are on Google Play, and Relay is a Mac app. The Switch Suite app for Android keeps them together in one clean dashboard, shows which ones you have, and opens them.

None of the apps show ads, and the Switch Suite app collects no personal data. See Privacy for details.
