About

Switch Suite is a family of small, focused Android utilities for people moving to a new phone ecosystem. Each utility has a familiar, minimalist design and is a separate app on Google Play. The Switch Suite app keeps them together in one clean dashboard, shows which ones you have, and opens them.

None of the apps show ads, and the Switch Suite app collects no personal data. See Privacy for details.
